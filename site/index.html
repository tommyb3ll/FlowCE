FlowCE

Calculus on your TI-84 Plus CE.

Type math the way it looks. Get exact answers the way your textbook writes them, and now the steps too.

Install Screens

Free and open source. For the TI-84 Plus CE and TI-83 Premium CE.

Install

You need a TI-84 Plus CE on OS 5.3 to 5.8.4, its USB cable, and Chrome or Edge on a computer. Installing erases the calculator's apps and archived files.

This browser can't reach a calculator. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or TI Connect CE.
On Windows: install TI Connect CE 6.1 or newer first. The page uses its USB driver; you don't need to open it.
  1. 1

    Connect

    Plug in the calculator and turn it on. Close TI Connect CE if it's open.

  2. 2

    Install

    Sends FlowCE.

  3. 3

    Press enter on the calculator

    When it shows INST, press enter once. It installs and restarts by itself ("RAM Cleared"). Then apps → FlowCE.

Install with TI Connect CE instead
  1. Clear the archive: 2nd + (MEM) → 7 → ▶ ARCHIVE → 3 → 2.
  2. Download FlowCE.b84 and send it with TI Connect CE.
  3. prgm → enter → enter opens arTIfiCE. enter starts INST, enter again installs.
  4. At "Success! Will now reset", press enter. Then clear, apps → FlowCE.
Trouble?
  • Connect fails on Windows: update TI Connect CE to 6.1 or newer, then unplug and replug the calculator.
  • Connect fails on a Mac or Chromebook: close TI Connect CE, then unplug and replug.
  • Linux: add a udev rule such as SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0451", ATTRS{idProduct}=="e008", TAG+="uaccess" in /etc/udev/rules.d/69-ti-calc.rules, then replug.
  • Still not enough memory after the erase: some apps survive it (often TI's language apps). Delete them: 2nd + → 2 → Apps, del on each.
  • "RAM Cleared" but this page still waits: FlowCE is installed. Press apps → FlowCE.
  • Frozen at "Success! Will now reset": press the reset button on the back (a pen tip). FlowCE stays installed.
  • The calculator acts strangely: clear the RAM: 2nd + → 7 → 1 → 2. FlowCE stays installed.
  • OS 5.8.5 or newer: not supported yet (the installer's arTIfiCE v2.1 works up to 5.8.4).

Something else, or a wrong answer? Tell us on the Cemetech thread.
